# 10 — References & Open Questions

## How to use references

Study **principles**, not layouts. The goal is not to look like these sites; it is to understand why they feel inevitable and apply that reasoning to Monalisha's work.

Reference categories:

1. **Spatial / 3D craft** — depth, camera control, materials, performance.
2. **Scroll storytelling** — pacing, sticky stages, transitions.
3. **Product clarity** — how real UI is presented and explained.
4. **Editorial / type** — hierarchy, restraint, captions, whitespace.
5. **Case study craft** — evidence, decisions, outcomes.

---

## 1. Spatial / 3D craft

| Reference | What to study | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Bruno Simon** | Playful 3D, physics, one continuous world | The gamification; this portfolio is not a game |
| **Aristide Benoist** | Restraint, camera control, material quality | The minimal content; we need evidence |
| **Dennis Snellenberg** | Smooth scroll-linked 3D, typography integration | Long intros; heavy scenes |
| **Active Theory** | Immersion, transitions, sound design | Complexity and spectacle |
| **Resn** | Experimental interaction, art direction | Novelty over clarity |
| **Unseen Studio** | Motion craft, pacing | Motion for its own sake |

**Takeaway:** the best 3D portfolios feel quiet. The camera moves less than you expect; the material and light do the work.

---

## 2. Scroll storytelling

| Reference | What to study | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple product pages** | Sticky product stages, outcome reveals, pacing | Length; repetitive pinning |
| **Locomotive** | Scroll choreography, editorial rhythm | Scroll hijacking |
| **Studio Freight** | Section pacing, type + motion | Heavy transitions on every section |
| **Linear** | Product storytelling with real UI | Feature-list density |
| **Stripe** | Systems/data visualization, clarity | Complexity |

**Takeaway:** one pinned moment is stronger than five. Release the pin before the reader is tired.

---

## 3. Product clarity

| Reference | What to study | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear** | UI presentation, density, states | Marketing gloss |
| **Raycast** | Product detail, keyboard-first craft | Dark-only aesthetic |
| **Vercel** | Clarity, performance, restraint | Monochrome coldness |
| **Stripe** | Systems thinking, diagrams | Enterprise heaviness |
| **Figma** | Feature storytelling with real UI | Long feature tours |

**Takeaway:** real screens, annotated decisions, and states beat abstract 3D. The product work should be the hero.

---

## 4. Editorial / type

| Reference | What to study | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Pentagram** | Type hierarchy, grids, restraint | Corporate case study tone |
| **Manual** | Editorial layout, whitespace | Distance from product work |
| **Basement Studio** | Bold editorial type, attitude | Neo-brutalist skin; we are moving away from it |
| **Studio Freight** | Type + motion integration | Over-animation |
| **AIGA Eye on Design** | Captions, pacing, editorial voice | Blog-first structure |

**Takeaway:** a warm, editorial frame makes product work feel considered. The type should feel chosen, not defaulted.

---

## 5. Case study craft

| Reference | What to study | What not to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe case studies** | Problem → system → outcome | Corporate scale |
| **Linear blog** | Decision-focused writing | Feature announcements |
| **Airbnb design** | Research + craft + outcome | Large-team scope |
| **Uber design** | Complex systems, operations | Brand gloss |
| **Independent designer portfolios** | Honest decision logs, small-team ownership | Generic process diagrams |

**Takeaway:** the best case studies read like a decision record, not a portfolio tour. One good trade-off is worth ten screens.

---

## Anti-references

Do **not** build:

- A 3D demo reel with a portfolio attached.
- A neo-brutalist template (the current site is the lesson).
- A dark-mode neon "creative developer" site.
- A horizontal-scroll experience.
- A page with a custom cursor, magnetic buttons, or scroll hijacking.
- A site that hides work behind an "Enter" gate or a 10-second loader.
- A gallery of Behance posters.
- A case study that offloads the real work to an external link.
- A "passionate product designer" template with five process diagrams.
- A site that requires WebGL to read.
- A site with 10px uppercase mono labels.
- A site with five testimonials and no outcomes.
- A site that copies a reference layout.

---

## Borrow / avoid

### Borrow

- The pacing of a great scroll story: one idea per viewport.
- The restraint of a great 3D site: slow camera, soft light, one material.
- The clarity of a great product site: real UI, states, annotations.
- The hierarchy of an editorial site: type, rules, captions, whitespace.
- The honesty of a great case study: decisions, trade-offs, caveats.

### Avoid

- Spectacle over evidence.
- Motion over meaning.
- Novelty over clarity.
- Trend over identity.
- Volume over depth.
- Claims without proof.

## Open questions

### Blocking before content lock

1. **Metrics:** what numbers exist for each of the three projects, and what can be shared?
2. **Permissions:** written permission for screens, logos, and quotes from Perx/Jabu?
3. **Role and team:** what was Monalisha's exact role, team size, and timeline on each project?
4. **Fact check:** is the 65% unbanked figure correct, and what is its original source?
5. **Employer naming:** can the engagement-platform project name Perx and its clients?

### Needed before design

6. **Availability:** start date, full-time/contract, remote scope?
7. **Geography:** Bengaluru, remote India, global remote, or relocation?
8. **Contact:** final email, LinkedIn, résumé PDF?
9. **Portrait:** new shoot or existing image?
10. **Research artifacts:** interviews, maps, clips, notes — what still exists?
11. **Prototypes:** any Figma prototypes or recordings worth embedding?
12. **AI work:** is there a real AI-assisted workflow to show on `/systems`?
13. **Systems numbers:** component counts, adoption, ship time.
14. **Testimonials:** which three, with photos and permission?
15. **3D assets:** Blender support, or build the scenes in code?
16. **Domain/email:** is `monalishachatterjee.com` owned and configurable? If not, use `monalisha-portfolio.vercel.app`.
17. **Analytics:** privacy-friendly (Vercel/Plausible) acceptable?
18. **Maintenance:** who edits content after launch?
19. **Notes:** essays in v1 or cut?
20. **Old site:** any URLs that need redirects?
